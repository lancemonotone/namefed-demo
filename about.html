About NAMEFED

Who We Are

North Adams M.E. Federal Credit Union was chartered January 1, 1959, in North Adams, Massachusetts. For over 60 years, we've served municipal employees and their families.

We're member-owned, not-for-profit, and focused on helping you save, borrow, and plan for the future—without the fees and hassle of big banks.

Who We Serve

NAMEFED membership is open to employees and retirees of:

  • City of North Adams
  • North Adams City Yard
  • North Adams Public Schools
  • North Adams Fire Department
  • North Adams Police Department
  • Northern Berkshire EMS
  • North Adams Housing Authority

Immediate family members can join!

Board of Directors

We have a volunteer Board of Directors that sets loan and dividend rates, amends policies, and makes important decisions for the Credit Union. Meetings are held on the second Monday of every month at 4:30 PM at the Credit Union office.

Board positions are open. If you're interested in serving, please call us at (413) 662-2657.
