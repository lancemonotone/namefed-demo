Who Can Join?

NAMEFED membership is open to municipal employees, retirees, and their families. If you work for the city, school district, transit authority, or public safety—or if you're related to someone who does—you likely qualify.

  • City of North Adams employees
  • North Adams Public Schools staff
  • North Adams Fire & Police Departments
  • Northern Berkshire EMS
  • North Adams Housing Authority
  • Municipal retirees
  • Immediate family of current members

What You'll Need

  • Driver's license or other valid government-issued picture ID
  • Social Security Card
  • Cash or check (minimum $5.00) made payable to NA ME Fed Credit Union

Once a Member – Always a Member! Even if you leave your employment or move out of the area, we just require annual activity in your account.
