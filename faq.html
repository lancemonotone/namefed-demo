Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about NAMEFED. Can't find what you need? Contact us—we're happy to help.

What is NAMEFED's routing number?

Our routing number is 211871234. Use it for direct deposit, wire transfers, and ACH setup. You'll also find it on your checks and in your account materials.

Who can join NAMEFED?

Membership is open to employees and retirees of the City of North Adams, North Adams Public Schools, North Adams Fire and Police Departments, Northern Berkshire EMS, North Adams Housing Authority, and North Adams City Yard. Immediate family members of current members may also join. See full eligibility details.

What are your branch hours?

We're open Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–4:00 PM. Summer hours (typically June–August): Monday–Thursday 9am–4pm, Friday 9am–1pm. All transactions must be in by 3:30 PM for same-day processing. View full hours and holiday schedule.

Where are you located?

We're at Norad Mill, 60 Roberts Drive, Suite 112, North Adams, MA 01247. Get directions and more details.

How much do I need to open an account?

Just $5.00 to open a Share Savings account. You'll need a valid government-issued ID, Social Security card, and cash or check made payable to NA ME Fed Credit Union. Apply for membership.

Do you offer online banking?

Not at this time. We're a small, branch-based credit union. You can visit us during business hours or call (413) 662-2657. We're exploring online banking for the future.

What if I leave my job or move away?

Once a member, always a member! We just require annual activity in your account to keep it open. You don't lose membership if you change jobs or relocate.

How do I set up direct deposit or payroll deduction?

Contact us for our routing number and your account number. We can provide a direct deposit form, or your employer may have their own. For payroll deduction on loans, we'll work with your employer once your loan is approved.

Is my money safe?

Yes. Your savings are federally insured to at least $250,000 by the NCUA (National Credit Union Administration), backed by the full faith and credit of the United States Government.

How do I apply for a loan?

Visit the branch or call (413) 662-2657 to speak with staff. We offer auto, personal, and seasonal loans. View current rates. We will match competitor rates with Board approval—just ask!
