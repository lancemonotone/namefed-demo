Welcome to NAMEFED

Chartered January 1, 1959, we've been serving North Adams municipal employees and their families for over 60 years. We're member-owned, not-for-profit, and focused on helping you save, borrow, and plan for the future.

Once a Member – Always a Member! Even if you leave your employment or move out of the area, we just require annual activity in your account.

Who We Serve

NAMEFED membership is open to employees and retirees of:

  • City of North Adams
  • North Adams City Yard
  • North Adams Public Schools
  • North Adams Fire Department
  • North Adams Police Department
  • Northern Berkshire EMS
  • North Adams Housing Authority

Immediate family members can join!

What We Offer

Savings & Christmas Clubs

Share Savings at a competitive rate. Just $5 to open. Christmas Clubs with checks printed October 1st. Lil Buck Savers for ages 0–17.

Loans

Auto, personal, motorcycle, utility trailers, snowmobiles, boats, campers, ATVs—plus seasonal loan specials throughout the year.

Payroll Deduction

Simplify the process and ensure payments are on time. Direct deposit available—choose any amount.
